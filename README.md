## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

The game uses a menu-driven structure rather than conventional free-roaming movement. Select bunker activities, survivor management screens, research options, or raid missions from the available interfaces. The trainer does not replace navigation; it removes delays and risks from the activities you choose.

### Tools & Inventory

Use the inventory and equipment screens to manage weapons, armor, and supplies for the survivor squad. Resource options are most useful before research, bunker upgrades, and equipment purchases. Activate unlimited supply features before spending resources, then turn them off when you want normal progression.

### World & Environment

The bunker serves as the main base for recruiting survivors, expanding facilities, researching technologies, preparing squads, and developing relationships. Raids take the team into the zombie-infested world to collect resources and advance the campaign. Unlock and progression options should be used between missions rather than during story transitions.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is built around deploying a squad against waves of zombies during raids. Equip survivors with suitable weapons and armor before starting a mission. Maximum health, increased damage, durability protection, and easier enemy waves can be combined for a low-risk run, while fast leveling and perk support help strengthen the team between raids.
